Under these experimental conditions, the leaching efficiencies achieved for Mn, Li, Co, and Ni were 99.31, 99.07, 98.64, and 99.31%, respectively. These results show that the elimination of secondary pollution from use of inorganic acids can be achieved without sacrificing high leaching efficiencies.
