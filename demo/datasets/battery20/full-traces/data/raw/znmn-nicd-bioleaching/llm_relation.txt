Although A. niger KUC5254 was the best at metal removal, it only extracted 32, 33, 13 and 1% of Ni, Cd, Co and Zn, respectively. Similar results were obtained for Zn–Mn batteries. It seemed that larger quantities of oxalic acid inhibited the dissolution of metals due to a precipitation reaction between Ni, Cd, Co, Zn, and oxalic acid (Biswas et al. 2013). Consequently, A. niger KUC5254, which showed the lowest oxalic acid production (4.4 mM), produced the highest percentage of metal removal.\nExtracted amounts of Ni, Cd, Co and Zn were definitely higher in sucrose media than in ME media.
