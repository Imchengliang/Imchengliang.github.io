    Data and statistical methods

    Records retain the reported values and units. Charts use a shared display unit and show measurements by component, feedstock and material system.

    In Visualization, each publication contributes one median. The overall median and range are calculated from these publication medians; the range spans their lowest and highest values. In Compare & Summarize, cross-study means and SD are shown for matching measurement contexts, with equal weight for each publication.
