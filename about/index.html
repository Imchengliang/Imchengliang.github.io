PhD Candidate @ University of Oslo — Knowledge Graphs, LLMs, and E-Waste Recycling

About Me

I'm Liang Cheng, a PhD candidate in the Department of Informatics at the University of Oslo. My research focuses on combining large language models and knowledge graphs to build intelligent systems for e-waste recycling and circular economy.

My PhD project — Efficient Recycling of E-Waste through Automated and Intelligent Resource Dataflow — explores how structured knowledge can be automatically extracted from scientific literature, product specifications, and unstructured data, then used to support decision-making in waste management and recycling.

Research Interests

  • Knowledge Graph Construction — ontology-guided LLM extraction, scientific document processing, table-to-KG
  • Large Language Models — synthetic data generation, quality assessment, multi-agent extraction pipelines
  • Graph Machine Learning — zero-shot link prediction, cross-domain transfer, structural role learning
  • E-Waste Recycling — WEEE domain knowledge systems, compositional data, recovery optimization
  • RAG / Expert Agents — domain-specific retrieval-augmented systems for scientific decision support
  • Applied ML — mixture density networks, transfer learning, dimensionality reduction
